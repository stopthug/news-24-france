He Trades Memecoins Every Day And Always Wins (Decu)

26 May 2010 · 864.2K views · Info Locale

A reader sent us the tape: a trader, known here as Decu, who says he trades memecoins every day — and always wins. No hedging. That is the claim.

The video went up on 26 May 2010. It has already passed 864,200 views. Info Locale publishes the account as it was given. We did not audit the wallets.

Memecoins are a gamble. Past wins are not a method. The figures in the video are the speaker’s, not ours.
